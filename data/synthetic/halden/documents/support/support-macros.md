## Directory Sync (SCIM) availability — updated 21 September 2026

"Directory Sync (SCIM 2.0 provisioning for Okta and Entra ID) is planned for general
availability in December 2026. A limited beta with selected customers starts in
November. Your account manager can share more detail about timing for your team."

Source: decision record of 18 September 2026. The previous version of this macro (August)
said "expected in October" and must no longer be used.

## Refund requests — updated 3 March 2026

"Enterprise subscriptions can be refunded in full within 14 days of the start of an
annual term. After that, Finance may be able to offer an account credit in some cases.
I've passed your request to your account manager, who will follow up."

Source: Enterprise refund policy v2.
