## About Halden

Halden makes Halden Flow, a data-sync and workflow-automation platform for mid-market
and enterprise companies. We are about 380 people, remote-first, with hubs in Boston and
Lisbon.

## How we work

- Written first: decisions go into decision records, plans into documents, and Slack is
  for conversation. If it matters, write it down where people can find it.
- Default to open channels. Private channels are for customer-confidential or
  personnel topics.
- Customers' names and data stay inside Halden's systems.

## Working hours

Core overlap hours are 14:00–17:00 UTC. On-call rotations are paid.

## Time off

25 days of paid time off plus local public holidays. Tell your manager two weeks ahead
for anything longer than three days.

## Security

Use Okta for every work app, keep MFA on, and report lost devices to IT within one hour.
