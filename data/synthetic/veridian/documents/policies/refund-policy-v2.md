Veridian Analytics. Effective 1 September 2025. Replaces refund policy v1.

## Refund window

Annual subscriptions may be refunded in full within **30 days** of the start of the term.

## After the window

Fees are non-refundable after 30 days. Service credits under a customer's SLA are not
refunds.
