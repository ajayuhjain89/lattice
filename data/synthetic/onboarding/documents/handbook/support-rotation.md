## Rotation

Wrenfield's support rotation changes every Monday at 09:00 UTC. The engineer on rotation
watches the shared queue and hands over in the support channel.

## Severity

Severity 1 incidents page the duty manager within 15 minutes. Severity 2 incidents are
answered within four business hours.
