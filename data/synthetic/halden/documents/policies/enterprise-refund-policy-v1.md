Owner: Finance Operations. Effective 1 February 2024. Applies to Enterprise contracts
paid annually in advance.

## Refund window

An Enterprise customer may request a **full refund within 30 days** of the start of a new
annual term (initial or renewal), for any reason.

## After the refund window

After 30 days, fees are non-refundable except where the Enterprise agreement provides
service credits (for example, SLA breaches).

## Seat reductions

Seats may be reduced only at renewal. Mid-term reductions are not refunded.

## Process

Account managers submit refund requests to Finance Operations (finance-ops@halden.example)
with the order form reference. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within
10 business days.
