## The opportunity

Orbital Freight is a freight forwarder with about 6,000 employees in 14 countries. They
run customs-document and carrier-booking workflows on Syncwell today and are unhappy with
its reliability. They are evaluating Halden Flow as a replacement. Expected deal: 4,500
Enterprise seats, about **$310,000 ARR**. Decision planned for mid-November.

The evaluation is confidential at Orbital's request: their Syncwell contract renews in
January and they do not want Syncwell to know before then. Keep Orbital's name out of
public channels and customer references.
