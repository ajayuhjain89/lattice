Veridian Analytics product decision. Status: approved 20 August 2026. Owner: Tariq Aziz.

## Decision

Veridian Insight will support SCIM 2.0 provisioning for Okta and Entra ID. A **beta starts
in October 2026** for customers who request it; **general availability is planned for
Q1 2027**.

## Why

Enterprise customers, including Acme Corp, need automated deprovisioning for audits.

## Communication

Support and account managers may share "beta in October, GA planned for Q1 2027".
