## Goals for Q3

1. **Okta cutover without disruption.** Acme moves Halden Flow SSO to Okta on 1 August.
   Success = zero lost logins on cutover day and no P1 tickets that week.
2. **Directory Sync adoption.** Acme expects SCIM provisioning in Q3 (kickoff call,
   12 June). Success = Acme's pilot group of 500 users provisioned through Okta before
   their SOX fieldwork.
3. **Audit log automation.** Extend the S3 audit log export to the finance workspace.
4. **Renewal readiness.** Renewal on 15 December 2026. Success = verbal commitment by
   31 October.

## Health

Green overall. Usage is growing (1,640 active users in March, 1,790 in June). The main
risk is timing: goals 1 and 2 both depend on dates Acme considers firm.
