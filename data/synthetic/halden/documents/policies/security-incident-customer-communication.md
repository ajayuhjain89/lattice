Owners: Head of Support and VP Engineering.

## During an incident

- Post the first status page update within 30 minutes of declaring an incident.
- Update the status page at least every 60 minutes until resolved.
- Do not name other affected customers to a customer.

## After an incident

- Publish a public incident summary within 5 business days for any incident longer than
  one hour.
- Enterprise customers may request a customer-specific root cause analysis; the account
  manager coordinates it with Engineering and shares it within 10 business days.
- Customer-specific RCAs are shared only with that customer and the internal account
  team.

## Security incidents

Security incidents that affect customer data follow the breach notification process:
affected customers are notified within 72 hours of confirmation.
