Released 18 August 2026 to all regions.

## Identity

- **SAML signing certificate rollover with overlap.** Halden now signs SAML requests
  with both the current and the next certificate for 30 days before a rotation, and
  shows a banner to workspace admins 14 days in advance. IdPs that refresh metadata pick
  up the new certificate automatically.
- SSO error pages now show a correlation ID for support.

## Flows

- New step: "Wait for approval" with Slack and email approvers.
- Scheduled flows can use workspace-local time zones.

## Connectors

- NetSuite connector v2 (saved searches, custom records).

## Fixes

- Webhook retry backoff now respects `Retry-After` headers.
- CSV imports keep leading zeros in text columns.
