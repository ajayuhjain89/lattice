Owner: Jonas Weber, Head of Support. Effective 6 January 2025.

## First-response targets

| Priority | Enterprise | Business | Team |
| -------- | ---------- | -------- | ---- |
| Urgent (P1) | **1 hour**, 24×7 | 4 business hours | 1 business day |
| High (P2) | 4 hours | 8 business hours | 2 business days |
| Normal (P3) | 1 business day | 2 business days | 3 business days |
| Low (P4) | 2 business days | 3 business days | 5 business days |

## Definitions

- P1: the service is unavailable or a core function fails for most users, with no
  workaround.
- P2: a core function is degraded or fails for some users.

## Escalation

P1 tickets page the on-call engineer immediately. P2 tickets open longer than 24 hours
are reviewed by the support team lead.
