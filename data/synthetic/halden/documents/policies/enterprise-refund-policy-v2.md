Owner: Finance Operations. Effective 3 March 2026. Replaces the Enterprise refund policy
v1 (February 2024). Applies to Enterprise contracts paid annually in advance.

## Refund window

An Enterprise customer may request a **full refund within 14 days** of the start of a new
annual term (initial or renewal).

## After the refund window

From day 15 to day 60 of a term, Finance may approve a **pro-rated account credit**
(not a cash refund) for unused months when the customer has a documented service
problem. After day 60, fees are non-refundable except for contractual service credits.

## Approvals

- Refunds and credits up to $25,000: Finance Operations.
- Above $25,000: the CFO.
- Account managers and support engineers cannot approve refunds or credits.

## Seat reductions

Unchanged: seats may be reduced only at renewal.

## Why this changed

Most v1 refunds were requested in days 20–30 after procurement-driven renewals, and cash
refunds late in the window were hard to forecast. Credits keep customers whole without
unwinding revenue.
