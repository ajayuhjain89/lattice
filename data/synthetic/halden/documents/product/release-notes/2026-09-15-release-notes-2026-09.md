Released 15 September 2026 to all regions.

## Data platform

- **Audit log streaming (beta).** Stream audit events to Splunk (HTTP Event Collector)
  or Datadog Logs within about a minute. Available to Enterprise workspaces on request;
  general availability is planned for the 2026.11 release.
- Flow run history search by status, trigger and date.

## Flows

- Parallel branches can now merge with "wait for all" or "first to finish".

## Admin

- Seat usage report shows last-login dates per user.

## Known issues

- Directory Sync (SCIM), previously planned for this release, is not included. Product
  will publish updated timing separately.
