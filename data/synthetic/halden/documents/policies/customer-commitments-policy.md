Owners: Grace Whitfield (VP Customer Success) and Tom Lindqvist (Product). Applies to
everyone who talks to customers: account managers, CSMs, solutions engineers, support.

## Roadmap statements

- You may share timing only from an **approved decision record** or the published
  roadmap, and you must say it is a target ("planned for", "expected in").
- Do not give dates from Slack conversations, standups or informal chats with engineers.
- If a customer needs timing that no decision record covers, ask Product in writing and
  tell the customer you will follow up.

## Commitments

A commitment is any statement a customer may reasonably rely on: a date, a feature, a
price or a service level.

- Feature or date commitments need written approval from Product (the decision owner).
- Price or credit commitments follow the discounting guidelines.
- **Contractual** commitments (anything in an order form, letter or email that promises
  a date or remedy) need VP Customer Success and legal review.

## When a date changes

The account manager tells affected customers within 10 business days of the decision,
and Support updates open tickets that quoted the old date.

## Record keeping

Record every commitment in the account brief with the date and who made it.
