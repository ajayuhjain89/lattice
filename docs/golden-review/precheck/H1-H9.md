# Engineering pre-check of the held-out labels (H1–H9)

Prepared on 2026-10-30 to speed up the owner's review (phase-13-followups §11.1). It
**approves nothing**: every item stays pending until the owner decides. Only held-out
items were read (`evals/golden/`); the sealed packets S1–S4 were not opened.

## Method

A script over the 126 held-out items, then a manual reading of every flag:
1. **Fact support.** Every salient token of each required fact must occur in the item's
   relevant, withheld or acceptable records, or in their titles, keys or dates. Salient
   tokens are identifiers, numbers, months and capitalised words.
2. **Answerable labels.** An `answered` or `partial` expectation with no relevant
   evidence or no required facts is flagged.
3. **Abstention labels.** For an `insufficient_evidence` expectation, a record the persona
   may read that contains every salient term of the question is flagged.

The script raised 11 flags. Nine were checked against the records and are fine.
