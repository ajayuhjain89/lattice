Blameless postmortem. Incident commander: Sam Patel. Author: Sofia Marchetti.
Shared with Support by department grant.

## Impact

SSO logins failed for workspaces whose IdP pins Halden's SAML signing certificate.
Five workspaces were affected, most heavily Kestrel Bank (about 3,100 users) and
Larkspur Retail (about 400 users). Password and API access were unaffected.

## Timeline (UTC, 21 July 2026)

- 07:38 — Annual certificate rotation job starts.
- 07:40 — First SAML validation errors in IdP logs.
- 07:52 — HAL-4702 (P1) from Kestrel Bank.
- 08:05 — Incident declared; Sam Patel incident commander.
- 08:40 — Cause identified: rotation replaced the certificate without dual-signing.
- 09:55 — Previous certificate restored; web logins recover.
- 10:15 — Mobile app sessions recover after cache expiry; incident resolved.

Total impact: **07:38–10:15 (2 h 37 min)** for mobile; web logins recovered at 09:55.

## Root cause

The rotation runbook's dual-signing step was skipped when the job was migrated to the
new scheduler in June. No alert fired on the SAML failure rate.
