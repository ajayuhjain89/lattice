Applies to new business, expansions and renewals from 1 February 2026.

## Approval levels (discount off list, annual contract value)

| Discount | Approver |
| -------- | -------- |
| Up to 10% | Account manager or account executive |
| 10–20% | VP Customer Success (renewals) or VP Sales (new business) |
| Over 20% | CEO |

Multi-year prepay may add up to 5 percentage points at the same approval level.

## Renewals

- Standard renewal uplift is 7%. Waiving it counts as a discount of the same size.
- Service credits are not discounts; they follow the Enterprise agreement and the refund
  policy.

## What not to do

- Do not trade discounts for roadmap dates.
- Do not quote a discount before it is approved, even as "likely".
