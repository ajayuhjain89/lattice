Draft for the leadership pricing review on 6 October. Not approved. Do not share with
customers or quote in deals.

## Proposal

- **Directory Sync becomes a paid Enterprise add-on** at $3 per user per month for new
  contracts and for renewals starting on or after 1 January 2027. Existing Enterprise
  customers who renew before that date keep it included for their next term.
- SSO stays included in Business and Enterprise.
- Audit log streaming: included in Enterprise; not available in Business.
- A new "Enterprise Plus" tier bundling Directory Sync, audit log streaming, EU residency
  and a 99.95% uptime SLA, at list +22%.

## Rationale

Identity features drove 40% of Enterprise win reasons this year. Competitors charge for
SCIM (Syncwell: $4 per user per month).

## Open questions

- Grandfathering for customers who were told SCIM would be included (at least Acme Corp,
  per the June kickoff notes).
- Whether Enterprise Plus cannibalises Enterprise renewals.
