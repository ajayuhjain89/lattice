Owner: Jonas Weber, Head of Support. Effective 12 January 2026. Replaces the 2025 SLA
policy.

## First-response targets

| Priority | Enterprise | Business | Team |
| -------- | ---------- | -------- | ---- |
| Urgent (P1) | **30 minutes**, 24×7 | 2 business hours | 1 business day |
| High (P2) | **2 hours** | 8 business hours | 2 business days |
| Normal (P3) | 1 business day | 2 business days | 3 business days |
| Low (P4) | 2 business days | 3 business days | 5 business days |

## What changed from 2025

- Enterprise P1 first response: 1 hour → 30 minutes.
- Enterprise P2 first response: 4 hours → 2 hours.
- Business P1 first response: 4 → 2 business hours.

## Definitions

Unchanged from 2025. P1: service unavailable or a core function fails for most users,
with no workaround. P2: a core function is degraded or fails for some users.

## Escalation

P1 tickets page the on-call engineer immediately and open an incident channel. P2 tickets
open longer than 8 hours are reviewed by the support team lead.
